ABOUT CODEX 4.0

Where logic meets competition.

CODEX 4.0 is an inter-college coding competition by Coders' Club, GPREC, designed to challenge problem-solving, collaboration and competitive programming skills.

Build. Think. Compete.

Teams work through two rounds of coding challenges, turning ideas and algorithms into efficient solutions under time pressure. The competition is structured to evaluate both logical problem solving and technical implementation speed.

2 Rounds CHALLENGE TRACKS
2–3 MEMBERS PER TEAM
Inter-College OPEN TO ALL COLLEGES

Built by Coders' Club

A student-driven technical community at G. Pulla Reddy Engineering College (GPREC) focused on coding, learning, collaboration, innovation, and creating opportunities for students to compete beyond the campus.

Through workshops, hackathons, and coding arenas, Coders' Club empowers passionate programmers to sharpen their skills and build real-world confidence.
