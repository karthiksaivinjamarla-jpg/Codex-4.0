FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got answers.

Find answers to commonly asked questions about team formation, eligibility, payment verification, and event logistics.

Who is eligible to participate in CODEX 4.0?

Students currently studying in 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year from any recognized engineering college are eligible to form a team. Each team can have at most one 4th-year student.

What is the team size and registration fee?

Teams can consist of either 2 or 3 members. The registration fee is fixed at ₹300 per team regardless of 2 or 3 members.

How does the payment and verification work?

Pay ₹300 using any UPI app (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM) via the QR code on the registration page, enter your 12-digit UTR number, and upload the payment receipt. You will immediately receive a digital confirmation pass and ticket stored securely in our database.

Can members of a team be from different branches or colleges?

Yes, cross-branch and cross-college teams are welcome as long as the eligibility criteria (maximum one 4th-year student) are met.

What should participants carry on the event day?

Every team member must carry their official College ID card and a copy of the Registration ID confirmation pass.

Can a student register in more than one team?

No. Each student (email, roll number, and mobile number) can only be registered with one team. The system automatically enforces uniqueness.
