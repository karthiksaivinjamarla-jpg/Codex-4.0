BEFORE YOU REGISTER

Know the rules.

Team size

Each team can have 2 or 3 members.

Registration fee

₹300 per team. The payment is completed during registration.

Eligible years

2nd Year, 3rd Year and 4th Year students are eligible.

4th-year rule

A maximum of one 4th-year student is allowed in a team.
