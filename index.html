01 // ABOUT

What is CODEX 4.0?

A competitive coding experience designed to bring bright minds together, solve meaningful problems and compete as a team.

Enter the arena.

CODEX 4.0 gives student teams an opportunity to put their programming, logic and problem-solving skills to the test across two rounds.

✓Team-based competitive coding
✓Problem-solving under time pressure
✓Inter-college participation
✓Recognition for top performers

Why participate?

Learn by competing, collaborate with your teammates and experience a focused coding challenge beyond the classroom.
