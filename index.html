ABOUT CODEX 4.0

Where logic meets competition.

CODEX 4.0 is an inter-college coding competition by Coders' Club, GPREC, designed to challenge problem-solving, collaboration and competitive programming skills.

Build. Think. Compete.

Teams work through two rounds of coding challenges, turning ideas and algorithms into efficient solutions under time pressure.

Built by Coders' Club

A student-driven technical community focused on coding, learning, collaboration, innovation and creating opportunities for students to compete beyond the campus.
